# Why can't I log in to an account I was recently added to?
{: #troubleshoot-join-account}
{: troubleshoot}
{: support}

Troubleshoot why you cannot log in to an {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} account that you were recently added to and learn how to accept or renew your invitation.
{: shortdesc}

You can't log in to an account you were recently added to.
{: tsSymptoms}

This problem can commonly occur for one of the following reasons:
{: tsCauses}

* You didn't accept the invitation to join the account.
* The invitation expired. You have 30 days to accept an invitation.
* The inviter canceled your invitation.

Complete the following steps to accept the invitation. If your invitation expired, contact the inviter or the account owner to request a new invitation.
{: tsResolve}

* If you're new to {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}}, you receive an email that contains all the information you need. Click the invitation link to join the account.
* As an existing member of {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}}, you can accept the invitation in your notifications, by email, or by using the CLI to onboard to the new account. To accept invitations in the CLI, use the [**`ibmcloud login`**](/docs/cli?topic=cli-ibmcloud_cli#ibmcloud_login) command.

If you completed all the steps and you still experience issues, contact [IBM Cloud Support](/unifiedsupport/supportcenter){: external}.
